# Support this work

The code is free. The measurements are the part that took the time.

Everything in this repository stays available: the ComfyUI nodes are GPL-3.0, and
`minimax_h3_latent_core` is free under PolyForm Small Business for individuals and small companies.
Sponsoring does not unlock anything - there is nothing held back.

What it pays for is the part that is expensive to produce: GPU hours spent establishing what
actually works. Every number in the README came from a run that had to be waited for, and most
of them came from runs that failed first.

- **Sponsor:** the button at the top of the repository (GitHub Sponsors)
- **Commercial licence:** see `COMMERCIAL.md`

## Where the numbers came from

One machine: RTX 3090, 24 GB, ComfyUI Desktop on Windows. Timings are from that GPU and will not
transfer directly to yours. Thresholds and behaviours should.

If you reproduce something and get a different answer, open an issue with your numbers. A
contradicted measurement is more useful than a confirmed one.
